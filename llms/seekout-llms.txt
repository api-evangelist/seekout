# SeekOut

> SeekOut is the agentic AI recruiting platform that helps talent acquisition teams source, screen, and engage candidates at scale. Used by 750+ leading customers including Microsoft, DocuSign, Sony, and Thomson Reuters.

## Products

- **SeekOut Recruit**: AI recruiting platform for sourcing, screening, and engaging talent. Access 1B+ candidate profiles with AI-powered search, inbound evaluation, and personalized outreach. https://www.seekout.com/solutions/recruit
- **SeekOut Spot**: AI recruiting service combining agentic AI with expert recruiters to deliver interview-ready candidates in 2 weeks at 70% lower cost than traditional agencies. https://www.seekout.com/solutions/spot
- **SeekOut Sam**: AI-powered inbound evaluation that reviews every applicant in seconds and surfaces best-fit candidates with explainable decisions. https://www.seekout.com/solutions/sam (free trial interview: https://www.seekout.com/try-sam)
- **SeekOut MCP**: Use SeekOut inside Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, and any MCP-compatible AI assistant. 14 built-in recruiting workflows across 6 data sources. https://www.seekout.com/solutions/mcp
